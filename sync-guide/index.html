How the member sync works, and how to test it

A guide for Alexa. The connector now keeps SmartMatch and GoHighLevel in step in both directions for 13 contacts. The technical checks are done. What is left is your business test.

In one minute

SmartMatchThe team works as usual. SmartMatch stays the source of truth.
⇄
ConnectorRuns on Maclynn's Cloudflare server, day and night. A pass every 2 minutes or so.
⇄
GoHighLevelContacts are created and updated. Edits made here go back to SmartMatch, within safe limits.
  • Timing: a pass takes about a minute and a new one starts about every 2 minutes. A change shows in the other system within 2 to 4 minutes. Wait 5 minutes before you call a test failed.
  • SmartMatch → GoHighLevel: every change of a synced field follows. New members are created.
  • GoHighLevel → SmartMatch: edits follow too, with the limits below.
  • No laptop needs to be on. The connector never sends a message.

The rules it always follows

  • EmailEmail is open in GoHighLevel only when the member said Yes to marketing emails and is not on the SmartMatch unsubscribe list.
  • Said noOtherwise email is blocked (Do Not Disturb).
  • Never reopensThe connector never reopens email in GoHighLevel. If SmartMatch goes back to Yes, reopening is a manual decision by the team.
  • SMS · WhatsApp · CallsAlways blocked.
  • SmartMatch winsIf the same field is changed in both systems at the same moment, SmartMatch's value is kept and GoHighLevel is set back to it.
  • ConsentsA No or a removed event type from either side is respected. A Yes only ever comes from SmartMatch.
  • One contactEach member has one GoHighLevel contact, linked by the Source ID. The connector never creates a duplicate.
  • When unsureThe connector holds the field and shows a line in the console rather than guessing.

How to test

Make changes only on the three TEST profiles. Write down the starting value before each change and put it back at the end. After each change, wait 2 to 4 minutes, then open and refresh the contact in the other system.

  1. A SmartMatch change reaches GoHighLevel

    On a TEST profile, change the city or the Cupid Club answer in SmartMatch. GoHighLevel shows the new value.

  2. A SmartMatch No closes email

    On a TEST profile, set marketing emails to No in SmartMatch. Email becomes blocked in GoHighLevel (DND tab).

  3. A GoHighLevel unsubscribe reaches SmartMatch

    On a TEST profile with email open in GoHighLevel, block Email in the DND tab. SmartMatch marketing emails turns to No.

  4. Email is never reopened by itself

    After step 2 or 3, set SmartMatch back to Yes. Email stays blocked in GoHighLevel. That is expected: reopening is your decision.

  5. Preference withdrawals reach GoHighLevel

    On TEST Connector One, set Cupid Club to No and clear the event types in SmartMatch. GoHighLevel shows No and an empty list. Then put the old answers back in SmartMatch.

  6. A GoHighLevel status reaches SmartMatch

    Give TEST Connector Two a profile status in SmartMatch (for example "Active Profile") and wait until GoHighLevel shows it. Then in GoHighLevel type another status exactly as SmartMatch spells it (for example "Inactive - Hold/Freeze"). SmartMatch follows. Set it back the same way.

  7. The rules fit Maclynn

    Do the fields, the consent rules and the closed channels match how Maclynn wants to use GoHighLevel? Tell Alexandra what to change.

Good to know

  • Real membersDuring the test, nobody edits the 10 real members in GoHighLevel: their GoHighLevel edits now reach SmartMatch too.
  • Same field, both sidesDo not change the same field in both systems within a few minutes. SmartMatch wins. After a pause, the console may not list such a field as "Changed in both systems" (a known display issue, the values are right): compare it by hand.
  • ClearingEmptying a name or city in one system does not empty the other. To clear it, clear it in both.
  • Back to YesTo turn Cupid Club or marketing emails back to Yes, change it in SmartMatch.
  • Status spellingProfile status in GoHighLevel is free text. Type it exactly as in SmartMatch, or the console shows "GHL holds a value SmartMatch does not accept" and nothing is written.
  • Event typesEdit them in SmartMatch. In GoHighLevel the field holds codes such as ["2","3"]: never type or import into it. If the console shows a problem on it, tell Alexandra.
  • Field namesDo not rename the GoHighLevel fields used by the connector (the ones starting with "SmartMatch").
  • CampaignsDo not start a GoHighLevel campaign or workflow on these 13 contacts during the test.
  • Nothing arrivedAfter 5 minutes, check that the console does not show Paused or an emergency stop, then send Alexandra the profile and the time of your change.
  • A line in the console"Write outcome unknown", "SmartMatch shows another value", "writes disabled" or "two contacts with the same email": tell Alexandra with the profile and the time.

Your checklist

  • A SmartMatch change shows up in GoHighLevel
  • Marketing No in SmartMatch blocks email, and Yes does not reopen it
  • A GoHighLevel unsubscribe turns SmartMatch marketing emails to No
  • Cupid Club No and cleared event types in SmartMatch reach GoHighLevel
  • A profile status typed in GoHighLevel reaches SmartMatch
  • The fields and rules fit how Maclynn wants to use GoHighLevel
  • No duplicate, no message, no unexpected change

If something looks wrong, note the SmartMatch number, what you expected and what you saw, with the time. Do not send screenshots of real members. Send it to Alexandra.
