How the member sync works, and how to test it

A guide for Alexa. What the connector does today, which contacts it keeps in step, and the checks to run before we add more members.

In one minute

SmartMatchThe team works as usual. SmartMatch stays the source of truth.
⇄
ConnectorRuns on Maclynn's Cloudflare server, day and night. Checks every member every minute.
⇄
GoHighLevelContacts are created and updated. An unsubscribe here is sent back to SmartMatch.
  • SmartMatch → GoHighLevel: new members are created, changes follow within a few minutes.
  • GoHighLevel → SmartMatch: only one thing goes back. When a member unsubscribes in GoHighLevel, "marketing emails" turns to No in SmartMatch. The connector never writes Yes, and never changes anything else in SmartMatch.
  • No laptop needs to be on. No messages are ever sent by the connector.

The rules it always follows

  • EmailEmail is open in GoHighLevel only when the member said Yes to marketing emails and is not on the SmartMatch unsubscribe list.
  • Said noOtherwise the contact is Do Not Disturb on every channel.
  • Never reopensOnce a channel is blocked in GoHighLevel, the connector never reopens it. Only the team can, by hand, after checking with the member.
  • SMS · WhatsApp · CallsAlways blocked.
  • PreferencesCupid Club and event types follow SmartMatch both ways (Yes or No), even while email is blocked.
  • One contactEach member has one GoHighLevel contact, linked by the Source ID. If an email already exists in GoHighLevel without a Source ID, the connector waits for an admin to approve the link. It never creates a duplicate.
  • Deleted or archivedThe contact is blocked in GoHighLevel. It is never deleted.
  • When unsureThe connector blocks and waits for an admin rather than guessing.

How to test

Already checked by Alexandra on 13 October: a city change on TEST Connector Two reached GoHighLevel within a minute. Make changes only on the three TEST profiles. After each change in SmartMatch, wait about three minutes, then open and refresh the contact in GoHighLevel.

  1. The 10 real members are there

    For each number in the list, find the contact in GoHighLevel. Check: one contact only, Source ID set, name, email, city and status match SmartMatch.

  2. Email follows consent

    For the same 10: if SmartMatch marketing emails is Yes, email is open in GoHighLevel. If it is No or empty, the contact is Do Not Disturb. SMS, WhatsApp and calls are blocked on all of them.

  3. A change follows

    On TEST Connector Two, change the city in SmartMatch and save. The new city appears in GoHighLevel.

  4. A preference follows, both ways

    On TEST Connector Two, set Cupid Club to No, then later back to Yes. GoHighLevel "SmartMatch Cupid Club" follows each time. Do the same with an event type.

  5. An unsubscribe in SmartMatch

    On TEST Connector Three (Alexandra reopens its email in GoHighLevel before your session), set marketing emails to No. The GoHighLevel contact becomes Do Not Disturb. Set it back to Yes: email stays blocked. That is expected.

  6. An unsubscribe in GoHighLevel

    Already checked on 13 October on TEST Connector Three: after Email was blocked in GoHighLevel, SmartMatch marketing emails turned to No within a minute. Nothing to do here.

  7. Nothing unexpected

    No new duplicate contact, no message sent, no other SmartMatch field changed.

Your checklist

  • The 10 real members are in GoHighLevel, once each, with the right details
  • Email is open only for members who said Yes
  • SMS, WhatsApp and calls are blocked on every contact
  • A city change on a test profile shows up in GoHighLevel
  • Cupid Club and event types follow SmartMatch both ways
  • Marketing No in SmartMatch blocks the contact, and Yes does not reopen it
  • A GoHighLevel unsubscribe turns SmartMatch marketing emails to No
  • No duplicate, no message, no unexpected change
