The Maclynn Connector

Keep working in SmartMatch. The connector keeps GoHighLevel up to date, so campaigns always reach the right members, and never reach someone who said no.

What it does

SmartMatchThe team updates a member's profile as usual.
→
ConnectorNotices the change and checks what the member agreed to.
→
GoHighLevelThe contact is created or updated. Campaigns use the latest details.

No copying, no spreadsheets, no double entry. Nobody needs to open GoHighLevel to keep it in step.

The rules it always follows

  • EmailEmail is open only when the member said yes to marketing emails.
  • UnsubscribeWhen a member says no, every channel is blocked in GoHighLevel straight away, even for a campaign already scheduled.
  • Never reopensThe connector never reopens a channel a member closed. Only the team can, after checking with the member.
  • SMS · WhatsApp · CallsAlways blocked, because members have not agreed to them.
  • One contactEach member has one contact in GoHighLevel, linked by their SmartMatch number. No duplicates.
  • When unsureIf anything looks wrong, the connector blocks and waits for the team rather than guessing.
