The new sales workflow

From the first conversation with a lead to a signed Private Membership Agreement, with Claude doing the typing and the team keeping the judgement. This replaces PandaDoc before 8 November. Prepared by Alexandra Spalato for Rachel, Alexa and Tory.

Today a proposal takes Tory between fifteen minutes and an hour and a half. She writes notes by hand, photographs them, types them up with ChatGPT, then fills in PandaDoc. Some meetings never get written up at all.

Rachel has already shown the better way. Last week she turned Tory's long letter into a short, beautiful proposal with Claude, and once the client asked for changes, turned it into the full agreement. This workflow makes that the normal route for every client, without the back and forth.

Conversation→ Notes→ Proposal→ Client's changes→ Agreement→ Signature→ New member

Step by step

Before and during the meeting
  1. Tory, in GoHighLevel

    1. NDA before the meeting

    The NDA never changes, so it stays a simple template in GoHighLevel, one per company (UK and US). Tory sends it from the lead's contact page.

  2. Tory and the note-taker

    2. The conversation is captured

    With the lead's permission, Granola or Plaud transcribes the meeting: face to face, on Zoom or Teams, or on WhatsApp. If the lead says no, Tory takes notes by hand as today. Her personal impressions stay handwritten either way. Choosing a note-taker compares the two tools and covers consent.

After the meeting
  1. Claude

    3. Notes in Tory's format

    Tory asks Claude for her notes. Claude reads the transcript through the note-taker's connector and writes the notes in the format Tory already uses: career, background, relationships, what the client is looking for. It also pulls out the deal points: length, fee, dates, anything special.

    The notes are filed on the contact in GoHighLevel. The profile details can later feed SmartMatch, so nobody types them twice.

  2. Claude

    4. A draft proposal

    Claude drafts the proposal from the notes, in Rachel's design and Maclynn's tone of voice. There can be versions for men and for women. Rachel can keep improving the design, and every salesperson gets the new version straight away.

  3. Tory

    5. Review and send

    Tory reads the draft and asks Claude for any change in plain words: "make this box bigger", "add the Christmas hold". She sends the PDF to the client by email from GoHighLevel, so it stays on the contact's record.

  4. Client and Tory

    6. The client's changes

    If the client wants something different, Tory pastes the points into Claude and gets an updated proposal. This repeats until the client is happy.

Contract and signature
  1. Claude

    7. The Private Membership Agreement

    Claude turns the agreed proposal into the formal agreement, combining the personal pages with the terms and conditions. The terms and conditions are locked: Claude never rewrites them. Only the personal parts change: introductions, length, fee, timeline, contact details.

  2. Claude, then Tory or Rachel

    8. A final check

    Claude compares the agreement with Maclynn's standard terms and flags anything that differs. Tory reads it. If a clause goes beyond the standard, Rachel approves it.

  3. Claude and BoldSign

    9. Signature

    Claude sends the agreement for signature through BoldSign, with the signature boxes already in place. The client signs on a computer or a phone. Signing contracts without PandaDoc explains how this works and why we chose BoldSign.

  4. Automatic

    10. A new member

    Once signed, GoHighLevel updates itself: the opportunity is marked as won, the signed PDF is attached to the contact and the matchmaker gets a task. The invoice goes out as the agreement states, with payment due on signing. The new member then moves into SmartMatch.

How it's set up in Claude

  • Brand guidelines for everyone. Rachel's brand and tone of voice become a shared guide in Claude, so every document sounds and looks like Maclynn.
  • A sales toolkit. A package added to Claude for the sales team, with three ready-made skills: one writes the meeting notes, one drafts the proposal, one turns it into the agreement. Each skill holds the instructions, the format and Rachel's design, so nobody has to explain them to Claude again. When a skill is improved, everyone gets the new version.
  • Connections. Claude connects to the note-taker, to GoHighLevel and to BoldSign, so nobody copies anything between tools.

The team stays on ChatGPT for a week or two while Rachel finishes the brand guidelines. The sales workflow moves to Claude first, because PandaDoc renews on 8 November.
