2. Three kinds of notes, one result

Transcript

Granola's recording of the whole conversation. Claude takes the facts and the client's exact words from it, and never adds anything that wasn't said.

Typed notes

What Tory types in Granola during the call: how the client came across, reservations, her feeling about the fit. Claude keeps these word for word as her impressions.

Handwritten

When there's no recording, or for impressions jotted down after the meeting. A photo in Claude is enough: it transcribes the page first and shows Tory what it read.

Tory's impressions are never mixed with the transcript. In the notes they appear on their own lines, starting "Salesperson's impression:", exactly as she wrote them. The more she types during the call, the fewer questions Claude asks afterwards.

3. Each meeting, step by step

  1. Tory · before the call

    Ask for consent and start Granola

    If the client says no, don't record. Take handwritten notes instead.

  2. Tory · during the call

    Type impressions in Granola

    A few words are enough. Granola keeps them with the transcript.

  3. Tory · after the call

    Put the note in "Sales calls"

    That's what lets Claude and HighLevel see it. Notes left in a personal folder stay private and can't be read.

  4. Tory · in Claude

    Ask for the write-up

    Claude finds the meeting in Granola by the client's name or the date. For handwritten notes, add the photo to the chat.

    "Write up my meeting with Oliver Ashdown from this morning."
    "Here are my handwritten notes from my meeting with Clara Whitfield. Write them up."
  5. Tory · in Claude

    Check the notes and answer the questions

    Claude checks for anything missing or assumed and asks only what the transcript and her notes don't cover.

  6. Automatic

    HighLevel gets the notes

    Once Tory has approved them, the notes are filed on the client's contact. Lines marked private stay out of the proposal.

5. What stays private

  • The key only reads the "Sales calls" space and other spaces where API access is on. Personal notes and private folders are never read.
  • Anything sensitive (health, family, money, a named ex, anything said in confidence) is marked private in the notes and never reaches a proposal.
  • Meeting notes stay in Claude and HighLevel. They're never sent by email or message.
