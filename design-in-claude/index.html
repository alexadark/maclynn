Changing your designs in Claude

The design system and the proposal have moved out of Claude Design · 29 October 2026

Where they areMake a changeSales toolkitKeep in mind

1. Where your designs are now

The Maclynn Brand Design System and the Maclynn proposal are now in Claude, on the Artifacts page. You can stop using Claude Design (claude.ai/design): the copies there are read-only, and the site closes on 14 December 2026.

Maclynn proposalClaude → Artifacts → Maclynn proposal.
Maclynn Brand Design SystemArtifacts page → Claude Design menu → View design systems. It opens in Settings; click its name. It's shared with everyone at Maclynn and set as the default.

2. Make a change

The proposal, or any design

Open it from Artifacts, then pick the way that suits the change:

  • Chat for anything broad.
  • Comments on one element for a precise change.
  • Direct edits on the page for small visual tweaks.
"On page 2, make the quote larger and move the photo to the right."
"Use the Beige Stone background on the matchmaker page."

There's no version history. Before a big change, ask Claude to keep the current version first:

"Save what we have, then try a version with the fee on its own page."

The design system

Its page can't be edited by hand yet. Open a chat and ask Claude for the change; it updates the design system for everyone.

"In the Maclynn design system, add this new photo to the imagery and use it for the proposal cover."

A new document

Start a design or a slide deck in Claude and choose the Maclynn design system from the design system menu. It's already the default.

3. How changes reach the sales toolkit

The sales toolkit keeps its own copy of the proposal design, so that every proposal and agreement comes out the same, and the check can read every page before an agreement goes for signature. A change you make in Artifacts doesn't reach it on its own.

  1. You change the design in Artifacts

    Until it looks the way you want.

  2. You send me the link

    With one line on what changed.

  3. I update the toolkit

    And send you a sample proposal and agreement as PDFs to check before the team uses it.

The agreement has no separate design. It's the agreed proposal, followed by the client's details, the Acceptance statements, the signatures and the Terms and Conditions. A change to the proposal design changes the agreement too.

The Terms and Conditions work the same way: once your lawyer has approved them, send me the Word file or a text PDF and I'll publish them in the toolkit.

4. Keep in mind

  • Open them now and then. An artifact nobody opens for your organisation's retention period can be deleted. Opening or editing it resets the clock.
  • Old chats and comments stay behind. They weren't moved. If you need anything from them, copy it before 14 December.
  • Other Claude Design projects, such as the Maclynn presentation template: tell me and I'll move them before 14 December.
