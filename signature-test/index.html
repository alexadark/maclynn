Test run: from a first call to a signed agreement

Today you play a Maclynn salesperson looking after a new client, Clara Whitfield. Follow along in your own Claude app: each step gives the sentence to type and what you should see. Prepared by Alexandra Spalato for Rachel, Alexa and Tori.

This is a test. Clara is made up. BoldSign only sends to Alexandra's test address, and HighLevel only updates the contact called TEST. Nothing reaches a real client and nothing signed today has legal effect.

Before we start

  1. Open the Claude desktop app, signed in with your Maclynn account.
  2. Click Cowork, then start a new task. No folder needed.
  3. Click +, then Connectors, and check Maclynn Signature is on.

Do every step in that same task, one message after another, as you would with a real client.

Good to know

  • Claude never sends anything unless you say so, and only after a READY check.
  • The terms and conditions are never rewritten for one client. Anything outside the standard goes to Rachel first.
  • If something looks wrong in a PDF, say it plainly ("the fee box is cut off"). Claude fixes it and shows you the new version.
