Test run: from meeting notes to a signed agreement

The step-by-step walkthrough for today's demo. Follow along in your own Claude app: each step gives the sentence to type and what you should see. Prepared by Alexandra Spalato for Rachel, Alexa and Tori.

This is a test. Everything runs in test mode with a made-up client, Clara Whitfield. BoldSign only sends to Alexandra's test address, and HighLevel only updates the contact called TEST. Nothing reaches a real client, and nothing signed today has legal effect.

Before we start

  1. Open the Claude desktop app and sign in with your Maclynn account.
  2. Switch to Cowork and choose a working folder, for example a new folder called Maclynn test on your desktop.
  3. In a new task, type / and check you can see the maclynn-sales skills: meeting-notes, proposal and agreement.
  4. Click the + button, then Connectors, and check Maclynn Signature is switched on.

If something's missing, say so straight away and we'll fix it before going further.

The walkthrough

  1. Everyone

    Write up the meeting

    Normally you'd start from a Granola recording or your own notes. Today we use the fictional meeting that comes with the plugin.

    Write up the meeting with Clara Whitfield, using the Clara Whitfield example from the maclynn-sales plugin.

    You should see: structured notes (Career, Background, Dating and relationships, What she's looking for, Deal points) and a short list of the deal facts.

  2. Everyone

    Draft the proposal

    Draft Clara's proposal.

    Claude first shows the commercial lines: £32,000, director-led search, 6 months active, 6 months hold, matchmaker Imogen Clarke. It writes nothing until you confirm.

    Yes, that's right.

    You should see: a five-page PDF in Rachel's design, women's variant, and a summary of what Claude used and left out. The private detail about Clara's parent must not appear.

  3. Everyone

    Ask for a change

    Try a real-life tweak so you see how revisions work.

    Clara would like to start in early December instead. Update the proposal.

    You should see: a version 2 of the proposal with the new start date, the fee unchanged.

  4. Everyone

    Prepare the agreement

    Clara has agreed to proposal v2. Prepare her Private Membership Agreement.

    Claude asks two things. Use the test details Alexandra gives on the call, never a real client's:

    • the client's email, for BoldSign;
    • who countersigns for Maclynn.

    You should see: a 13-page agreement and a check report. READY means the terms and conditions are word for word the reference version and every figure matches the proposal. NEEDS RACHEL lists what Rachel has to approve first.

  5. Alexandra, on the shared screen

    Send it for signature

    Only one person sends during the test, since BoldSign accepts only the test address. Watch this step on the shared screen.

    Send Clara's agreement for signature.

    Claude asks for the HighLevel contact, opportunity and matchmaker to update. It never guesses them. Then it uploads the PDF and sends it.

    You should see: a confirmation with a document number, the signing order (client first, then Maclynn) and the mode sandbox.

  6. Alexandra, on the shared screen

    The client signs

    The BoldSign email arrives. The client opens it, fills in their details on page 6 (name, address, country, postcode, mobile, email, date of birth), then signs and dates page 7.

    You should see: the countersigner gets their email automatically once the client has signed.

  7. Alexandra, on the shared screen

    Maclynn countersigns

    The countersigner opens their email and signs. That completes the agreement.

  8. Everyone

    Follow up from Claude

    Where does Clara Whitfield's agreement stand?

    You should see: Completed, both signatures done, and three HighLevel updates confirmed.

  9. Everyone, in HighLevel

    Check HighLevel

    • The TEST opportunity has moved to Won.
    • A note starting Signed agreement links to the signed PDF.
    • A Start matchmaking task is waiting for the matchmaker.

    Nobody touched HighLevel by hand.

Good to know

  • Claude never sends anything unless you say so in the conversation, and only after a READY check.
  • The terms and conditions are never rewritten for one client. A request to change them becomes a special condition that Rachel approves in writing.
  • If something looks wrong in a PDF, tell Claude in plain words ("the fee box is cut off"): it fixes it and shows you the new version.

Before real clients

  1. Rachel's approved contract files (UK, New York, Los Angeles, the Private Membership Agreement and the Terms & Conditions) added to the contracts project. The UK terms used today are a provisional version.
  2. The Maclynn countersigner and the default matchmaker for the follow-up task decided.
  3. Rachel's go-ahead, then Alexandra switches BoldSign from test to live.
