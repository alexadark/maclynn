Why change

  • PandaDoc costs a lot for how we use it: about 800 of the 1,800 credits we pay for get used.
  • The template is locked. Only a few fields can change, while Rachel wants a free, personal design for each client.
  • The Monday integration doesn't fill every field, so Alexa and Tory finish documents by hand.
  • Rachel has already proven the new format with Claude: a short, beautiful proposal first, then the full agreement once the terms are agreed.

Why not GoHighLevel's own signing

We tested it on Rachel's draft agreement. GoHighLevel imports the PDF perfectly, design intact, and a signature box can be added on page 7.

  • Signature boxes can only be dragged in by hand, and that has to be done again for every contract.
  • The editor ignores the boxes until all 16 pages have loaded, which takes around twenty seconds. It's easy to think it's broken.
  • GoHighLevel's API can't create a document or place the boxes, so Claude can't do it for us.

GoHighLevel stays the CRM and still receives every signed contract. Only the signing step moves to a specialist tool.

How it will work

  1. Tory and Claude

    Claude prepares the agreement

    From the approved proposal and the terms agreed with the client, Claude writes the Private Membership Agreement. The terms and conditions never change. Tory reads it and asks for any tweaks in the chat.

  2. Claude and BoldSign

    Claude sends it for signature

    Claude sends the final PDF to BoldSign with the position of each box: the client's signature and date on page 7, and Maclynn's countersignature if we keep one. Nobody drags anything.

  3. The client

    The client signs

    They get an email in Maclynn's colours, open the link and sign on a computer or a phone. BoldSign keeps the evidence: time stamps, IP address and a signing certificate.

  4. Tory or Rachel

    Maclynn countersigns

    If we keep a countersignature, it goes out automatically once the client has signed, in the order we set.

  5. Connector and GoHighLevel

    GoHighLevel updates itself

    As soon as the contract is signed, BoldSign tells our connector. The connector marks the opportunity as won, attaches the signed PDF to the contact and creates the matchmaker's task.

  6. Tory and Claude

    Follow-up from Claude

    Tory can ask Claude where a contract stands, send a reminder or find a signed PDF, without opening BoldSign.

What we still need to settle

  • Where the signature sits. Today's PDFs are pure images, so the boxes are placed by position, and the signing page has to stay in the same place. Once Claude builds the PDF itself, hidden tags will mark the boxes and the design can move freely.
  • Legal check. Maclynn's lawyer should confirm that a standard electronic signature is enough for this agreement.
  • PandaDoc archive. Export every signed contract before the subscription ends.
